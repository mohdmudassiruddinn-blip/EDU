🔒 Privacy

Your money stays on your phone

Everything you type in EDU (Every Day Use) is saved only on your own phone or computer, inside this browser. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that stores your information.

No account, no tracking

You don't need to sign up or give your name, email or phone number. The app has no ads and no tracking.

People and phone numbers

Names and phone numbers you add in the People section stay on your phone too. The Call and Remind buttons simply open your own phone or messages app.

More than one person, and PIN

Several people can use EDU on one phone. Each person has a separate money book. A PIN, if you set one, is stored only as a scrambled code on your phone. If you forget it, it cannot be recovered, but you can start again or bring back a backup.

Photos and sharing

Photos you add to a payment (a receipt or a screenshot) are saved only on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap 📤 Share and choose an app yourself, like WhatsApp. Backups include your photos.

Backups

A backup file is saved to your own device when you press "Make a backup". You decide where to keep it. Anyone who has that file can open your entries, so keep it safe.

Deleting your data

Press "Delete everything" in Settings, or remove the app and clear this website's data in your browser settings.

Not financial advice

EDU helps you keep track of your own money. Its answers, like "Can I buy this?", come from the numbers you type in. It is not financial, tax or legal advice.

EDU loads its letter style (font) from Google Fonts. Google may see that a font was requested, but no money information is ever sent.
